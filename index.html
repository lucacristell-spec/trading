The Bottleneck Theory

Markets reward scarcity. The scarcest resource in AI is not compute — it's the physical infrastructure that makes compute possible.

Structural Constraint

The bottleneck is physical and cannot be solved by software. Power, cooling, interconnect — these require years of infrastructure buildout.

⚡

Non-Substitutable Demand

AI cannot scale without these components. There is no alternative to optical interconnect at scale. No alternative to liquid cooling for 100kW racks.

👁️

Under-Radar Suppliers

The market obsesses over NVIDIA. The real alpha is in the suppliers NVIDIA cannot operate without — and that most investors have never heard of.

📈

Duration Mismatch

Wall Street thinks in quarters. Infrastructure buildouts take years. This creates a structural edge for patient capital.
